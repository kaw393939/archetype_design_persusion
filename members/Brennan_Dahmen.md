## My archetype and design choices

Chosen archetype: The Sage.

AI suggested that I am a Sage, and I agree. The Sage values wisdom, curiosity, and understanding, which matches how I like to approach life and work. I enjoy asking questions, comparing different ideas, and looking for the reasoning behind each option before making a decision. I appreciate explanations that simplify complexity and make information easier to understand. For my personal page, the Sage archetype gives me a direction that feels thoughtful, calm, and focused on learning.

Imagery, color, and type cues: I want to use images of books, research, libraries, and simple diagrams to communicate discovery and understanding. A palette of deep navy, white, and muted gray will create a composed, focused appearance. I can pair classic serif headings with readable sans-serif body text and use generous spacing to make the content easy to follow.

Modern or postmodern style: A modernist design approach fits the Sage best because it emphasizes clarity, structure, and function. Clean grids, consistent typography, and restrained decoration help visitors focus on the information instead of being distracted by unnecessary visual noise. A postmodern approach could still support curiosity and alternative thinking, but for this design direction, modernism is the stronger choice because it feels organized, trustworthy, and easy to navigate.

Headline: "Explore Ideas. Build Understanding."

CTA and destination: "Explore the Resources" — leads to a resource page with educational articles, guides, and research organized by topic.

Persuasion principle and why it fits: Authority. The Sage builds trust through demonstrated knowledge, clear explanations, and reliable evidence. Providing thoughtful insights and useful information makes visitors more likely to see the page as credible and helpful.
