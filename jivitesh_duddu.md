## About me
[](https://github.com/mnguyenht/Minh_-archetype_design_persusion/blob/main/members/jivitesh_duddu.md#about-me)
My name is Jivitesh Duddu, and I am drawn to creative work that blends imagination with practical execution. I enjoy exploring ideas through games, apps, UI/UX, stories, technology, business, content, and marketing. I like building things in my own way, learning from experimentation, and shaping rough ideas into something people can actually use or enjoy.

I am motivated by the process of turning a concept into a finished experience. I value creativity, problem-solving, and the freedom to try new approaches. Through this project, I want to strengthen my ability to communicate my ideas clearly and present my work in a way that reflects who I am and what I want to build.

## My archetype and design choices
[](https://github.com/mnguyenht/Minh_-archetype_design_persusion/blob/main/members/jivitesh_duddu.md#my-archetype-and-design-choices)
Chosen archetype: The Creator.

AI suggested that I am a Creator, and I agree. The Creator archetype reflects my interest in originality, experimentation, and making things that feel personal and purposeful. I enjoy building ideas from scratch, testing different directions, and turning concepts into experiences that other people can interact with. I do not just want to follow what already exists; I want to explore new ideas and make them my own.

Imagery, color, and type cues: I want to use visuals that suggest process and invention, such as sketches, prototypes, interface details, game-inspired graphics, and behind-the-scenes work. A palette of retro-game red with comic-inspired black and gray creates a bold, energetic feel. I can pair expressive headlines with a modern, clean sans-serif for body text so the page feels creative without becoming hard to read.

Modern or postmodern style: A modern or postmodern approach works well for The Creator because it allows experimentation while still keeping a clear structure. I want the page to feel inventive, energetic, and distinct, but not chaotic. A balanced modern aesthetic will help the ideas stand out while maintaining usability and readability.

Headline: "Build it your way. Make it real."

CTA and destination: "Explore My Work" — leads to a project gallery or portfolio page highlighting prototypes, experiments, and finished creative work.

Persuasion principle and why it fits: Authority with a creative edge. The Creator builds trust through strong examples of work, visible process, and proof of execution. Showing real projects and explaining how ideas evolved makes the page feel credible and inspiring.
