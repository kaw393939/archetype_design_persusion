# The Innocent Archetype

## What It Is

The Innocent is a brand archetype centered around optimism, simplicity, honesty, and happiness. Innocent brands try to create a sense of trust and positivity by presenting themselves as genuine, wholesome, and dependable.

The Innocent archetype often appeals to people who value simplicity and want products or services that feel safe and straightforward.

## When to Use It

The Innocent archetype works well for brands that want to communicate trust, positivity, simplicity, and reliability.

It can commonly be used by brands related to:

- Food and beverages
- Family products
- Health and wellness
- Personal care
- Household products
- Products aimed at creating comfort or happiness

A brand may use the Innocent archetype when it wants customers to associate it with honesty, familiarity, and positive experiences.

## How to Apply It

### Imagery

Innocent brands can use imagery involving:

- Nature
- Families
- Smiling people
- Bright environments
- Simple backgrounds
- Everyday positive experiences

Images should generally feel natural, welcoming, and uncomplicated.

### Colors

Light and clean colors can help communicate the Innocent identity. Examples include:

- White
- Light blue
- Yellow
- Light green
- Pastel colors

These colors can help create associations with cleanliness, optimism, simplicity, and happiness.

### Typography

Innocent brands can use clean and simple typography that is easy to read. Fonts should generally appear friendly and approachable rather than aggressive or highly decorative.

### Wording and Tone

The language of an Innocent brand should be positive, simple, friendly, and reassuring.

Examples of phrases include:

- "Enjoy the simple things."
- "Made with care."
- "Simple. Honest. Good."
- "A little happiness every day."

The tone should feel optimistic, trustworthy, welcoming, and sincere.

## Examples

### Dove

Dove often uses approachable messaging and imagery centered around everyday people, care, and simplicity.

### Coca-Cola

Coca-Cola frequently uses positive imagery and messaging involving happiness, friendship, and shared experiences.

### Aveeno

Aveeno uses nature-focused imagery and straightforward messaging about gentle personal-care products.
