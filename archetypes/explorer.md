# The Explorer Archetype

## What It Is

The Explorer is a brand archetype centered around freedom, independence, discovery, and adventure. Explorer brands encourage people to leave their comfort zones, experience new things, and discover more about themselves and the world around them.

The Explorer archetype often appeals to people who value independence and do not want to feel restricted. Its message focuses on creating your own path rather than simply following others.

## When to Use It

The Explorer archetype works well for brands that want to promote adventure, independence, discovery, or personal freedom.

It is commonly used by brands related to:

- Travel and tourism
- Outdoor activities
- Vehicles
- Sports and recreation
- Adventure equipment
- Lifestyle products

A brand may choose the Explorer archetype when it wants customers to associate its products or services with discovering new experiences and having greater freedom.

## How to Apply It

### Imagery

Explorer brands can use imagery involving:

- Mountains
- Forests
- Open roads
- Trails
- Oceans
- Camping
- Travel
- Outdoor environments

Images showing large, open environments can help communicate freedom and exploration.

### Colors

Natural and earthy colors can support the Explorer identity. Examples include:

- Green
- Brown
- Blue
- Gray
- Tan

These colors can create associations with nature, travel, and outdoor environments.

### Typography

Explorer brands can use simple and bold fonts that are easy to read. Strong sans-serif fonts can create a modern and adventurous appearance.

Typography should generally feel practical and confident rather than overly decorative.

### Wording and Tone

The language of an Explorer brand should encourage independence and discovery.

Examples of phrases include:

- "Find your own path."
- "Discover what is out there."
- "Explore beyond the ordinary."
- "Your next adventure starts here."

The tone should feel adventurous, confident, independent, and curious.

## Examples

### The North Face

The North Face is an example of a brand that uses themes associated with exploration and adventure. Its products are designed for outdoor activities, and its branding frequently uses outdoor environments and adventurous experiences.

### Jeep

Jeep frequently associates its vehicles with freedom, off-road travel, and adventure. Images of Jeeps traveling through mountains, trails, and other outdoor environments support characteristics associated with the Explorer archetype.

### Patagonia

Patagonia's outdoor clothing and equipment are closely connected with outdoor recreation and exploration. Its branding frequently features natural environments and outdoor activities.
