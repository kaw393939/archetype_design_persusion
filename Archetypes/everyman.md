# Everyman Archetype

## What is it?

The Everyman archetype represents belonging, connection, equality, and the desire to be accepted as part of a community. Rather than presenting itself as superior or exclusive, the Everyman emphasizes familiarity, approachability, and shared experiences.

It connects with audiences by focusing on experiences and needs that people share. In branding and communication, the archetype can be used to create a sense of trust and belonging.

Its central idea can be summarized as: **“I belong here, and so do you.”**

## How do you recognize or use it?

The Everyman archetype can be recognized through communication that emphasizes familiarity, accessibility, and belonging. Rather than making the audience feel that they need to be exceptional or different, the design creates a sense of connection by focusing on shared experiences.

Common characteristics include:

- **Relatable people:** Using realistic people and situations that the intended audience can recognize.
- **Approachable language:** Using simple, conversational, and easy-to-understand language.
- **Inclusiveness:** Communicating that the product, service, or organization is welcoming to ordinary people.
- **Familiar settings:** Using everyday environments and situations that feel recognizable to the audience.
- **Shared experiences:** Focusing on common needs, activities, challenges, or experiences.
- **Practicality:** Presenting the product or service as useful and accessible rather than exclusive.
- **Community:** Emphasizing relationships, friendship, family, teamwork, or a sense of belonging.

The Everyman archetype can be used by brands and organizations that want to establish trust and connect with a broad audience.

## Examples

### IKEA
IKEA can be associated with the Everyman archetype because its business idea emphasizes making well-designed and functional home furnishings affordable to as many people as possible. IKEA describes this approach as "Democratic Design," which combines form, function, quality, sustainability, and low price. This emphasis on accessibility and everyday life reflects the Everyman characteristics of practicality, inclusion, and belonging. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

### Target

Target can also be associated with the Everyman archetype. The company emphasizes accessible style and design, with affordability and accessibility as important parts of its brand strategy. Target describes its products as affordable and designed for everyday use, which connects with the Everyman's emphasis on accessibility and serving a broad audience. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
