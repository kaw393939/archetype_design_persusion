# Swiss Style / International Typographic Style

## What it is
Swiss Style is a modernist design style that became popular in Switzerland during the 1950s. It focuses on clean layouts, strong typography, grids, simplicity, and clear communication. The style avoids unnecessary decoration and instead uses structure and organization to make information easy to understand.

## Main Features
- Clean grid-based layouts
- Sans-serif fonts
- Lots of white space
- Strong alignment
- Simple shapes
- Minimal decoration
- Clear visual hierarchy

## Why I Chose It
I chose Swiss Style because it looks clean, modern, and professional without being too dramatic. I like how it keeps everything organized and visually attractive while still being simple.

## How I Would Use It
I would use Swiss Style for websites, posters, portfolios, or professional branding. I would use a clean grid, large sans-serif headings, simple colors, and plenty of spacing to make the design easy to read and modern.
